About DocuFlow

A Minimal Corporate Dashboard with a Cozy Digital Notebook feel

What is DocuFlow?

DocuFlow is a document intelligence dashboard that automatically categorizes and formats your text inputs — whether they're emails, legal contracts, or casual meeting notes — into beautifully structured, easy-to-read outputs.

How it works

  1. Paste your document text into the editor
  2. Select the document type (or let auto-detect figure it out)
  3. Process to generate a formatted analysis
  4. Export as PDF or copy the formatted result

Design Philosophy

Built with a Corporate Dashboard Minimalist aesthetic layered with a Cozy Warm Digital Notebook feel — warm cream tones, amber accents, and serif headings that make professional documents feel approachable.

Themes

Switch between Light Mode (warm cream + charcoal + amber) and Dark Mode (deep espresso + warm off-white + muted gold) using the toggle in the sidebar.
